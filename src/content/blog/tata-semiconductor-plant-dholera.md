**Quick answer:** Tata Electronics is building India's first commercial semiconductor wafer fab in Dholera SIR, Gujarat, with technology partner **PSMC (Taiwan)**. The **Union Cabinet approved it on 29 February 2024** at **₹91,000 crore** and **50,000 wafer starts per month**. The PM laid the foundation stone on **13 March 2024**, and the Centre notified a **66.166-hectare SEZ** for it on **9 April 2026**. The fab will make chips from **28 nm to 110 nm**, starting with **55 nm and 90 nm**. Construction is under way; MeitY has revised earlier production-date guidance.

## Why 55/90 nm first is not "bad news"

Mature nodes (55–110 nm) make the chips found in **cars, EV power systems, industrial controllers, meters, appliances and power-management ICs**. India imports large volumes of these, and supply shortages of exactly these chips stopped car production worldwide in 2021–22. Starting with well-understood processes lowers risk. **28 nm**, used in many communications and consumer chips, follows once yields are stable.

## What the fab means for Dholera, and what it doesn't

**What it does:**
- Gives Dholera a **globally recognised industrial anchor** with central fiscal support.
- Brings suppliers that would not come otherwise (gases, chemicals, equipment service).
- Creates demand for **skilled housing, rentals, schools, healthcare and retail** in and around the activation area.
- Gives weight to the airport, the expressway and the semi-HSR plans.

**What it does not do:**
- It does **not guarantee** price rises for every plot in a 920 sq km region. Effects are strongest close to built infrastructure and weaker farther away.
- It does **not** make up for poor plot paperwork. NA status, clear title and approved layout still decide whether a plot is a sound asset.
- It does **not** shorten the overall city timeline. The DSIR is planned in phases over many years.

## FAQ

**When will the Tata semiconductor plant in Dholera start production?**
The plant is under construction. MeitY revised earlier production-date guidance at Semicon India in September 2026. Judge progress by the SEZ notification, visible site work and supplier build-outs, not by a single brochure date.

**How much is Tata investing in the Dholera fab?**
₹91,000 crore, as approved by the Union Cabinet on 29 February 2024.

**Who is Tata's partner?**
Powerchip Semiconductor Manufacturing Corp (PSMC) of Taiwan provides the technology. Tokyo Electron is the equipment and training partner.

**What chips will be made in Dholera?**
Chips on 28–110 nm nodes. Production starts with 55 nm and 90 nm (power management, automotive, industrial), and 28 nm follows for higher-performance uses.

**How many jobs will the fab create?**
The SEZ notification lists about 21,000. Tata has said 20,000+ direct and indirect skilled jobs. Supplier and service jobs come on top of that.

**How big is the Tata fab site?**
The notified SEZ is 66.166 hectares (about 163 acres).

**Is the fab inside the Dholera activation area?**
Yes. It is in the developed Phase-1 zone of Dholera SIR, which has trunk roads and utilities.
