## 1. Dholera at a glance

> **Dholera** is a planned industrial smart city in Ahmedabad district, Gujarat. It is officially called the **Dholera Special Investment Region (Dholera SIR or DSIR)**. It covers **920 sq km**, which is larger than Singapore, and is the largest city being built under the **Delhi–Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC)**. The first 22.5 sq km **activation area** already has roads, water, power and drainage. In 2026 the **Ahmedabad–Dholera Expressway** opened, the **Tata–PSMC semiconductor fab** site was notified as an SEZ, a **semi-high-speed rail line** was approved, and the first aircraft landed at **Dholera International Airport**.

## 3. Why infrastructure comes first: the Dubai lesson

Dubai did not start with towers. It started with a port (Jebel Ali, 1979), a free zone (JAFZA, 1985), an airline and a highway. The skyline along Sheikh Zayed Road came last, after roughly three decades of infrastructure.

Dholera follows the same order: legal framework, then trunk utilities, then expressway, airport and anchor industry. The comparison helps explain *how planned cities grow*. It does not predict prices. Governance, capital, tax rules and demand drivers in India are very different from Dubai's.

## 4. Dholera and the Delhi–Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC)

The **DMIC** is India's first planned industrial corridor. It began with an India–Japan understanding in **December 2006**, received in-principle Union Cabinet approval in **August 2007**, and gained its implementing company, **DMICDC (now NICDC)**, on **7 January 2008**. Dholera was approved as a DMIC node in **May 2008**.

| DMIC fact | Verified figure |
|---|---|
| States covered | 6: Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, Maharashtra |
| Estimated programme size | ~US$90–100 billion over many years |
| India–Japan partnership | NICDC is 74% held by Indian public bodies (GoI 49%, HUDCO 19.9%, IIFCL 4.1%, LIC 1%) and 26% by Japan's JBIC¹ |
| Freight backbone | Western Dedicated Freight Corridor, Dadri–JNPT, ~1,506 km. **Fully completed 8 Sep 2026** (JICA)² |
| Road backbone | Delhi–Mumbai Expressway: 1,386 km main alignment. 929 km open as of March 2026 (MoRTH)³ |
| Planned nodes | 24: 11 Investment Regions + 13 Industrial Areas. **Dholera is the largest**⁴ |

Dholera is not on the Delhi–Mumbai Expressway itself. It connects through the dedicated **Ahmedabad–Dholera Expressway** and Gujarat's state highway network.

<a id="master-plan"></a>
## 6. Legal framework and master plan

Dholera is a statutory city with its own law, authority and development company. Private layouts do not define it.

| Layer | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Law** | Gujarat Special Investment Region Act, 2009 (in force 6 Jan 2009) |
| **Notification** | Dholera SIR notified May 2009: 920 sq km, 22 villages |
| **Regulator** | DSIRDA (Dholera Special Investment Region Development Authority), constituted 16 Feb 2010 |
| **Development company** | DICDL, incorporated 28 Jan 2016: 51% Gujarat (via DSIRDA, contributing land) / 49% Centre (via NICDIT, contributing funds) |
| **Planning model** | Land pooling under Town Planning (TP) Schemes. TP1–TP6 cover **422 sq km**. Draft TP schemes were published in 2012 |
| **Activation area** | **22.54 sq km in TP2**, developed and operational. The CCEA approved ₹2,784.83 Cr of trunk infrastructure for it in May 2015 |
| **Administration** | Dholera is its own taluka of 33 villages, 22 of them inside the SIR⁵ |

**Land-use mix** in the Development Plan includes industrial, residential, commercial, logistics, knowledge/IT, recreation and green zones, plus a dedicated city centre. Before buying, check which zone and TP scheme a plot falls in. That decides what can be built on it.

## 13. Dholera FAQ

### What is Dholera?
Dholera is a planned greenfield industrial smart city in Ahmedabad district, Gujarat, officially called the Dholera Special Investment Region (Dholera SIR). It covers 920 sq km and is the largest city being developed under the Delhi–Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC).

### Where is Dholera located?
Dholera is on the Gulf of Khambhat coast in Ahmedabad district, Gujarat, about 83 km (around 45 minutes) from Ahmedabad via the Ahmedabad–Dholera Expressway.

### Is Dholera bigger than Singapore?
Yes. Dholera SIR covers 920 sq km. Singapore's land area is roughly 728–735 sq km.

### Who is developing Dholera SIR?
The city's infrastructure is developed by Dholera Industrial City Development Ltd (DICDL), owned 51% by the Government of Gujarat and 49% by the Government of India. The Dholera Special Investment Region Development Authority (DSIRDA) is the regulator. Private developers build residential and commercial plotted projects in and around the SIR.

### What is the Dholera activation area?
The activation area is the first developed zone of Dholera SIR, about 22.54 sq km in Town Planning Scheme 2. It has trunk roads, water treatment, effluent treatment, stormwater drains, power, ICT networks and the ABCD administrative building.

### Is the Ahmedabad–Dholera Expressway open?
Yes. The Ahmedabad–Dholera Expressway opened to traffic in February 2026 and was inaugurated on 31 March 2026. The trip from Ahmedabad to Dholera takes about 45 minutes.

### When will Dholera International Airport start operations?
The runway, taxiway and air traffic control are complete, and the first calibration aircraft landed in June 2026. Operations are targeted for late 2026, subject to DGCA licensing.

### Is the Tata semiconductor plant in Dholera confirmed?
Yes. The Union Cabinet approved the Tata Electronics–PSMC fab on 29 February 2024 at ₹91,000 crore, and a 66.166-hectare SEZ for it was notified on 9 April 2026. Construction is under way in the activation area.

### Which other companies are investing in Dholera?
Tata Power (300 MW solar) and ReNew (solar cell and module manufacturing) are operating. INOX Air Products and Linde are building gas facilities for the fab. L&T Vyoma, Jabil, Nextgen Hitech, Grew Energy and Adani–Embraer have signed MoUs, and India and the UAE signed a Letter of Intent for Dholera in January 2026.

### Is there a railway to Dholera?
The Union Cabinet approved the 134 km Sarkhej–Dholera semi-high-speed rail line on 13 May 2026, at a cost of ₹20,667 crore and a design speed of 200 km/h.

### Can individuals buy land in Dholera?
Yes. Individuals usually buy non-agricultural (NA) residential or commercial plots from private developers in and around the SIR. Industrial land inside the SIR is allotted by DICDL, mainly to companies.

### What is the price of a plot in Dholera?
Prices depend on location, road frontage and distance to the expressway, airport and activation area. Plots in Mirrikh Infratech projects offered by Capital Brix start from ₹7,250 per sq yd, plus development, registration and legal charges.

### Can NRIs buy plots in Dholera?
Yes. Under FEMA rules, NRIs and OCIs can buy residential and commercial non-agricultural plots in India. They cannot buy agricultural land, farmhouses or plantation property. Payment must come through normal banking channels, such as NRE, NRO or FCNR accounts.

### What documents should I check before buying a Dholera plot?
Check the NA order for the survey number, a title search with no encumbrances, the 7/12 extract, the approved layout and zoning, and make sure the purchase is completed by a registered sale deed.

### Is investing in Dholera guaranteed to give returns?
No. Infrastructure progress in Dholera is real and verifiable, but land prices depend on location, market cycles and project execution. No investment is guaranteed. Verify each plot's documents and choose a holding period that suits your goals.

### Sources and notes

**How we verify:** This page draws on the *Dholera Awareness Program* (the seminar presented by Mirrikh Infratech). We check each figure against official records (PIB, NICDC, DICDL/DSIRDA, JICA, MoRTH) and company releases. Where the awareness materials and official records differ, we use the official figure and note the difference below.

**Deck vs official figures**
1. **DMIC funding:** The awareness deck says "74% Government of India via DMIC Trust / 26% Japan". In NICDC's shareholding, the 74% is the combined total of Indian public holders (GoI 49%, HUDCO 19.9%, IIFCL 4.1%, LIC 1%), and JBIC holds 26%.
2. **Western DFC:** The deck says "operational 31 March 2026". The final sections were dedicated on 8 September 2026 (JICA), completing the ~1,506 km corridor. The deck gives 1,504 km.
3. **Delhi–Mumbai Expressway:** The deck says 1,480 km with 1,169 km (~78%) operational. MoRTH told Parliament in March 2026 that the main alignment is 1,386 km (1,445 km with spurs), with 929 km open. More may have opened since.
4. **DMIC nodes:** The deck's "11 industrial nodes" refers to the 11 Investment Regions out of 24 planned nodes.
5. **Dholera taluka:** The deck dates the taluka to 26 January 2015. Gujarat announced the new taluka in September 2013, and 2015 may be when it became functional.
6. **Expressway distance:** The deck says 71 km. PIB gives a total expressway length of ~109 km and an Ahmedabad–Dholera trip of 83 km / ~45 min. We use PIB.
7. **Airport runway:** The deck says 4,000 m. The Phase-1 runway is 3,200 m (CAPA). 4,000 m matches the long-term master plan. The deck's operations date has been brought forward to late 2026 (target).
8. **Semi-HSR:** The deck adds a ~48-minute journey and 284 villages connected. PIB's approval gives 134 km, 200 km/h and ₹20,667 Cr, which are the figures we use.
9. **Solar park:** The deck says 4,400 MW, and some websites quote other figures. GPCL's official plan is 5,000 MW (1,000 MW + 4,000 MW).
10. **Fab jobs and output:** The deck says 26,000 direct + 1 lakh indirect jobs and 300 crore chips/year. PIB's SEZ notification lists ~21,000 jobs. We found no official source for the chip count. The deck's production date has since been revised by MeitY; see our [Tata fab guide](/blog/tata-semiconductor-plant-dholera) for the latest official guidance.
